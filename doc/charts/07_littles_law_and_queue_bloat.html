07. Little's Law ($L = \lambda W$) & The Queue Bloat Trap

At fixed arrival rate λ = 100 RPS, when downstream response time $W$ spikes from 50ms to 2000ms, concurrent in-flight demand $L$ surges from 5 to 200 concurrent requests. If large waiting queues are allowed to form, requests age in the queue until caller context deadlines expire before execution begins.
