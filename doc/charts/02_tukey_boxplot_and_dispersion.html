02. Tukey Five-Number Summary & Outlier Dispersion

Tukey's summary decomposes response times into non-parametric boundaries: Q1 (25th percentile), Median (50th percentile), and Q3 (75th percentile). Outliers are mathematically defined as any observation exceeding Q3 + (1.5 * IQR).
