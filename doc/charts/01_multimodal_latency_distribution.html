01. Multimodal Latency Distribution & Fallacy of the Mean

In distributed architectures, latency is multimodal: 95% of requests hit the cache fast-path (10ms), while 5% stall on a database lock (2,000ms). The arithmetic mean of 109.5ms lies in an empty valley—it describes no user's actual journey.
